01 — About

From buildings
to games

I first got into programming as a hobby while studying and practicing architecture and construction engineering, writing scripts and plugins to streamline the software I used for personal and professional work. That grew into building web applications, both for my employer and for my own projects, and now I'm branching out into game development.

Outside of code, I've always been drawn to creative work: writing, performing and recording my own music, and drawing, painting and sculpting. Game development feels like a natural place where all of those interests meet. Combining my coding experience (and, more recently, AI tools) with my love of art and music, I set out to make games that are genuinely fun to play.

My biggest inspiration comes from the RPGs I grew up with on the Game Boy, SNES and N64.
